My Work

Rich Men Of Dhobi Ghat

This video takes you inside the heart of Mumbai’s iconic Mahalakshmi Dhobi Ghat, looking past the grit to reveal a thriving community built on incredible hard work. It’s a powerful story of resilience, showing how a century-old tradition has evolved into a massive operation that quietly keeps the entire city running. What’s most moving is seeing how the sweat and dedication of these workers have paved the way for their children to become the doctors and engineers of tomorrow. It’s a beautiful reminder that there is immense dignity in every trade and that the most humble beginnings can build the brightest futures.

Belagavi's Milk Magic

The video explores the legacy of Belagavi Kunda, a traditional sweet discovered by accident over 130 years ago when milk was left to boil for too long. It spotlights Atul Purohit, who stepped away from a career in the Hindi film industry to preserve his family’s culinary heritage. The documentary details the labor-intensive process of stirring milk for hours to reach its signature amber color and flavor. Purohit’s transition from cinema to the sweet shop brought a unique discipline and focus to the traditional craft. By prioritizing honesty and quality, the business has remained a trusted icon in a competitive market. This story highlights the intersection of cultural identity, endurance, and the integrity required to sustain a multi-generational legacy.

Mumbai's Pav Bhaji

Discover the untold story behind Mumbai’s iconic Pav Bhaji—a dish that began in the 1860s to feed tired mill workers, and today brings everyone together, from billionaires to daily wage earners. This film takes you behind the steaming tawas of two legendary spots—Cannon, known for its strong, women-led team, and Sardar, where generous slabs of butter are part of the experience. More than just street food, this is a story of people, hard work, and the spirit that keeps Mumbai going.

Kozhikode's Halwa

This documentary captures the century-old legacy of Shankaran Bakery, a cornerstone of Kozhikode’s culinary tradition since 1933. The film explores the evolution of Calicut Halwa from its 12th-century Arabic roots to its status as a Malabar icon, defined by pure coconut oil and a labor-intensive stirring process. By highlighting the bakery’s refusal to use artificial ingredients or mass-production shortcuts, the narrative celebrates a fourth-generation family commitment to quality and historical preservation.

Moti Mahal's Butter Chicken

This documentary explores the origins of Butter Chicken at Delhi’s iconic Moti Mahal, tracing its evolution from a 1947 kitchen innovation into a global culinary phenomenon. It features Vinod Chadha, who revived the legendary establishment in 1992, highlighting his dedication to preserving the original "Murgh Makhani" recipe amid modern legal battles over its invention. Blending history with a deep respect for culinary tradition, the film captures how this single dish became a symbol of India’s cultural and gastronomic identity.

Goa's Azulejos

This documentary traces how Azulejos evolved in Goa from a 16th-century Portuguese craft into a vibrant expression of local identity. It highlights artist Shanka Turi, who expanded the traditional blue-and-white tiles into 115 colors inspired by Goan life, with collaborations including Mario Miranda. Blending old kiln techniques with modern creativity, these hand-painted tiles continue to tell Goa’s cultural story.

Stone Sculpture

This documentary showcases the meticulous craftsmanship of the Vishwakarma and Gudigar communities in South India, highlighting their dedication to the ancient arts of stone and sandalwood carving. It explores the spiritual and technical journey of transforming raw materials into sacred deities, preserving a 5,000-year-old cultural heritage through precision and devotion.

Davanagere Benne Dose

Davanagere Benne Dose is not just food, it is the taste of a town’s history. Born in the 1920s, it carries the soul of firewood kitchens and timeless technique. Crisp on the outside and delicate within, each dosa reflects generations of skill. Its unmistakable aroma comes from pure village butter. Every plate tells a century-old story of culture.

Kinnal Art

This documentary focuses on Santhosh Kumar, an artist dedicated to preserving and evolving the traditional Kinnal art of Karnataka. It showcases how he blends age-old techniques with modern ideas to give the craft a fresh and contemporary identity. Through his journey, the film highlights his passion, creativity, and commitment to keeping this heritage art alive for future generations.

Kasaragod Silk Factory

The video about Kerala's Kasaragod Silks Factory shows elderly artisans who, despite their age and struggles, continue to weave with dedication and pride, keeping the rich tradition alive through their hard work and resilience.

Kerala's Coconut Coir Industry

The video about Kerala's Coconut Coir Industry highlights how skilled workers transform coconut husks into eco-friendly coir products, showcasing Kerala's traditional craftsmanship and sustainable livelihood practices.

Navalgund Durrie

The video about Navalgund Durrie showcases the traditional handwoven cotton rugs made by women artisans in Navalgund, Karnataka, highlighting their vibrant geometric patterns and cultural heritage.
